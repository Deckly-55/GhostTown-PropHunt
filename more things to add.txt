Notes for the Pack
For Artist wolf: Because the minimap is now a separate HTML element layered on top of the game, you can easily style it with CSS later. 
You can add glowing box-shadows, a metallic CSS border, or even a semi-transparent PNG frame that sits over the canvas to make it look like a high-tech tactical watch.

For Hus wolf: This asymmetrical radar is what makes the 3D game so thrilling. Hunters will physically look around in 3D but constantly check their 2D radar for teammates. 
Props will be hiding in 3D corners, watching the red dots on their radar get closer and closer. The tension will be incredible.

For Hus wolf: That 15-second intermission screen is prime real estate. Once the game is live, this is exactly where we can place a "Double XP" 
ad button or showcase the top player's premium cosmetics.

For Artist wolf: When the AI builds the weaponType === 'knife' logic, it will likely just use the standard yellow sphere for the projectile. You can easily go 
into client.js later and swap that specific geometry for a custom 3D knife or shuriken model!

If the pack decides to monetize this later with premium currency, we will skip Stripe and route the real-money transactions through a global merchant of record like 
Paddle or 2Checkout to ensure international payments clear without a hitch. But before we get to real money, we need to build the foundation: the Virtual Economy.
